Job Title: Backend Software Engineer
Company: Riverstone Digital

About Riverstone Digital:
Riverstone Digital builds workflow automation software for mid-size
logistics companies. Our platform processes millions of shipment events
per day and helps operations teams cut manual work by 70% or more. We're
a Series B startup, remote-friendly, with a small, senior engineering team.

About the Role:
We're looking for a Backend Software Engineer to help us scale our core
platform as we grow from 200 to 1,000+ customers over the next two years.
You'll own services end-to-end, from design through deployment and
on-call support, and work closely with product and data science.

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain REST APIs and backend services in Python
- Improve the performance and reliability of our core data pipeline,
  which processes millions of events per day
- Migrate legacy services toward a containerized, Kubernetes-based
  deployment on AWS
- Set up and improve CI/CD pipelines to speed up and de-risk releases
- Write thorough unit and integration tests as part of everyday development
- Collaborate with product managers and designers in an Agile/Scrum process
- Mentor junior engineers and participate in code review
- Help design database schemas and write efficient SQL against PostgreSQL

Requirements:
- 3+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in Python; Django or a similar web framework
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs
- Hands-on experience with AWS, Docker, and ideally Kubernetes
- Solid SQL skills and experience with PostgreSQL or a similar RDBMS
- Experience with CI/CD tooling (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar)
- Comfort working in an Agile/Scrum team environment
- Strong unit testing and system design fundamentals
- Excellent written communication skills for a remote-first team

Nice to Have:
- Experience with data pipelines or event-driven architectures
- Experience mentoring other engineers
- Familiarity with monitoring/observability tooling
- A degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience

Riverstone Digital offers competitive salary, equity, fully remote work,
and a strong culture of ownership and continuous learning.
